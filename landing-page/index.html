Code of the Road

Our rules

Every driver is expected to follow these guidelines to keep the fleet safe and professional.

  • Follow all official TruckersMP rules at all times.
  • Respect every player — whether a VTC member or not.
  • In-game tag must show "Shakti Logistics" with the designated RGB colour.
  • Keep TrucksBook (or compatible tracker) running while driving to log activity.
  • You may only represent Shakti Logistics, not any other VTC, on this account.
  • Report any active TruckersMP ban to staff immediately via Discord.
  • If leaving, inform staff in Discord beforehand.
  • No trolling, ramming, toxic behaviour, or disrespectful conduct.

Full rulebook is available in our Discord server. Violations result in staff action.
